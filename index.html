    How it’s calculated

    • Every visit, test and fill is run through each plan’s in-network rules: per-person and family deductibles (shared-only on F4 and my spouse’s HDHPs), copays before or after the deductible, coinsurance, and per-person and family out-of-pocket maxes.
    • Any setup with an HDHP assumes the HSA is maxed out: tax savings = 30% × (contribution limit − employer money). The 2027 limit is $9,000 when anyone in the household has family HDHP coverage (spouse & kids, or me & kids on F4), and $4,500 when the only HDHP is self-only coverage (spouse alone, or me alone on F4). Adults are assumed to use the employer clinic for primary care on my spouse’s plans ($0).
    • My spouse’s Reach and Focus Medium copays are assumed from the Flex Medium plan summary. My spouse’s Low plans use the >$75k terms. My F4 has no known employer HSA contribution.
    • Max = premiums + every plan’s out-of-pocket max, after HSA money and tax savings. Out-of-network care, non-covered services and balance billing aren’t included.
